# Privacy Policy for NoteLedger

**Last updated:** 2026

## 1. Overview
**NoteLedger** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is developed with a strict **offline-first and privacy-by-design** architecture. We believe your personal notes, financial records, budgets, and daily expenses belong exclusively to you. 

## 2. Information Collection and Use
- **Personal Data:** We do **not** collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or contacts.
- **Notes and Expense Entries:** All notes, tags, categories, expense logs, payment methods, and timestamps you create are stored **100% locally on your device** using a local SQLite/Room database. None of this data ever leaves your device or touches external servers.
- **Backups and Exporting:** When you export JSON, CSV, or PDF files, the files are written directly to your chosen local device storage. We have no access to these exported files.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services
The Application integrates **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements. 
- Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) and device diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable laws.
- For more information on how Google processes data, please visit [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. Permissions
The Application requests minimal Android system permissions:
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required solely for loading Google AdMob advertisements and verifying network status.
- **WAKE_LOCK:** Used by Android components to complete local database operations smoothly.
No sensitive hardware permissions (such as GPS Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or SMS) are ever requested or accessed.

## 5. Security of Your Data
Because your data is stored solely on your local device, the security of your notes and expenses is protected by your device's operating system security (Android sandbox and device PIN/biometrics). We recommend keeping your device protected with a passcode or biometric lock.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
