## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy
**NoteLedger** ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), published by **Barshon Hossain**, is built from the ground up with a strict **offline-first and privacy-by-design** architecture. We firmly believe that your personal notes, journals, financial records, budget plans, daily expenses, and debt tracking belong exclusively to you. 

Our official developer website and home for all product information, documentation, and policy updates is **https://mobiledoctor12.blogspot.com**.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Zero Personal Data Collection:** We do **not** collect, harvest, profile, monetize, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, photos, or contact lists.
- **100% On-Device Local Storage:** Every note, journal entry, expense amount, transaction category, payment method, budget goal, debt record, and timestamp you author is stored **strictly locally on your Android device** in a private SQLite database managed by Android Room. None of this data is ever uploaded to external servers, cloud providers, or third-party databases.
- **Backups & Exporting:** When you export your data to CSV, JSON, or printable document formats, the files are written directly into your chosen local device storage via Android's secure `FileProvider`. We have zero access to your exported files.

---

## 3. Advertising & Tracking Status (ADS STOPPED)
- **Ads Completely Disabled:** All in-app advertising, banner ads, and interstitial ads have been **completely stopped and disabled** across the Application.
- **Zero Ad Tracking:** Because ads are disabled, no ad requests are made, and no user activity, advertising identifiers (AAID), or profiling cookies are tracked or transmitted to advertising networks.

---

## 4. Third-Party Diagnostic Services
- **Firebase Crashlytics & Diagnostics:** To detect unexpected application crashes, Application Not Responding (ANR) events, and device fragmentation issues across Android releases, the application may collect anonymous diagnostic logs and stack traces. These technical telemetry reports contain only device hardware architecture and OS version information—never user notes, expenses, or financial figures.

---

## 5. Android System Permissions
The Application uses the minimum system permissions required to function:
- **`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:** Allows opening external developer web links (such as visiting https://mobiledoctor12.blogspot.com in your browser) and transmitting anonymous crash diagnostics.
- **`touchscreen` (Required Feature):** Restricts the app to standard handheld touchscreen devices (smartphones and tablets), eliminating Android TV or auto targets.
- **Zero Dangerous Permissions:** The Application **never** requests or requires dangerous permissions such as `CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `READ_CONTACTS`, `READ_SMS`, or broad storage access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children.
